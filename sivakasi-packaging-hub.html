Sivakasi’s Leading Packaging Manufacturer – Bobby Packaging

Looking for a premier packaging partner in Tamil Nadu? Bobby Packaging, founded by Aakash Arun and based in the vibrant industrial city of Sivakasi, is the trusted manufacturer of primary packing bags, specialty films, and custom barrier pouches.

Sivakasi is globally recognized as the capital of printing and packaging in India. Bobby Packaging harnesses this rich industrial legacy, combining modern extrusion technology with high-speed flexographic printing to deliver packaging that protects your products and makes your brand shine on store shelves.

From bakery covers and salt bags to industrial rolls and high-barrier detergent pouches, we deliver wholesale packaging solutions that meet the highest standards of safety, quality, and aesthetics.

Our Sivakasi Infrastructure & Manufacturing Lines

Our manufacturing plant at the Sivakasi Industrial Estate is equipped with cutting-edge machinery:

  • Blown Film Extrusion Lines: State-of-the-art multi-layer extruders handling virgin LDPE, LLDPE, and HM-HDPE resins with precise gauge control.
  • Flexographic Multi-Color Printing: In-house plate making and high-speed flexo presses for vivid brand logos and regulatory information.
  • Precision Slitting & Rewinding: High-speed slitting lines producing tight, uniform rolls for automated form-fill-seal machines.
  • Automated Bag Sealing & Cutting: Bottom-seal, side-seal, and pouch-making machines ensuring airtight, leak-proof joints.

Nationwide Distribution from Sivakasi

Situated with direct road and rail connectivity across South India, Bobby Packaging provides reliable transit schedules to:

  • Tamil Nadu: Daily dispatch to Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tirupur, Salem, and Trichy.
  • Kerala & Karnataka: Regular freight to Kochi, Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Calicut.
  • Pan-India Supply: Bulk containerized dispatch to major commercial and industrial centers nationwide.
