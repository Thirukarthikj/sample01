Our Custom Manufacturing Process

How we bring your custom packaging vision to reality:

  1. Requirement Analysis: Understanding your product characteristics, barrier needs (moisture, aroma, light), and shelf-life targets.
  2. Material & Micron Specification: Expert recommendation of virgin polymer grades, multi-layer laminates, and gauge thicknesses.
  3. Flexographic Pre-Press & Plate Making: High-definition graphic prepress and precision polymer plate manufacturing for razor-sharp artwork.
  4. Extrusion & Precision Printing: Consistent blown film extrusion and multi-color printing under strict quality inspection.
  5. Slitting, Conversion & Quality Control: Automated pouch forming, hermetic seal validation, and rigorous tensile testing before dispatch.
