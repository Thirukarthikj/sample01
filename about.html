Our Foundation

Crafting Reliable Packaging with Innovation & Integrity

Bobby Packaging was established in the industrial powerhouse of Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, under the visionary leadership of Aakash Arun. Driven by a commitment to quality and technological excellence, we specialize in manufacturing primary packing bags and specialty films that safeguard product freshness while enhancing retail brand visibility.

From bakery bread covers and heavy-duty salt sacks to industrial continuous LD rolls and multi-layer detergent pouches, we combine high-tech blown film extrusion machinery with precision multi-color flexographic printing. Our facility adheres to stringent quality parameters, ensuring consistent micron thickness, high tensile elasticity, and dependable heat sealing.
